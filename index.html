Heltec V4 Mac SE

A 1 MB Macintosh SE: System 4 to 6, 800K and 1.44 MB disks. Uses the board's own USB, so no driver is needed.

Open the V4 Mac SE flasher

How it works

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to USB boards; Firefox and Safari can't).
  2. Plug in the board with a USB-C cable that carries data, open the flasher, click Connect, then Flash.
  3. Join the board's hotspot, open http://192.168.4.1, and add your Wi-Fi, ROM and disks.
